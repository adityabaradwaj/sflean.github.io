# Sponsor the SF Lean Meetup

We host weekly talks on Lean, formal methods, AI, and math, Mondays at 7pm at [Mox SF](https://moxsf.com/). About 20 people come each week (engineers, mathematicians, AI researchers), and every talk goes up on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCAA5ZP3KJkVjjJfJeZuCfiA).

Sponsors help us pay for food and video editing.

| Package | Amount |
|---|---|
| Food for a quarter | $3,000 |
| Video editing for a quarter | $2,500 |
| Food for one night | $300 |

**You get:** your logo on our videos, a thank-you at the meetup and on Luma, and a few minutes to say what your team is working on or hiring for.

Organizers choose all talks. Interested? Email [adityabaradwaj97@gmail.com](mailto:adityabaradwaj97@gmail.com?subject=Sponsoring%20the%20SF%20Lean%20Meetup).

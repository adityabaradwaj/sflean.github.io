# Sponsor the SF Lean Meetup

The SF Lean Meetup hosts weekly talks and discussions about Lean, formal methods, software verification, AI, and math. We meet Mondays at 7pm at [Mox SF](https://moxsf.com/) (1680 Mission St), and have been running since October 2025.

We're looking for sponsors to help with two things: food on meetup nights, and an editor for the talk videos.

## Who comes

Our audience includes software engineers, mathematicians, AI researchers, and economists, among others. Some work on Lean or formal verification full-time, some are learning it, and many are somewhere in between. Each week is a mix of regulars and new faces.

- **In the room:** around 20 people each week.
- **On Luma:** recent talks have had 30–75 people registered.
- **Online:** every talk is recorded and posted to [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCAA5ZP3KJkVjjJfJeZuCfiA) and Twitter/X, and linked from [our homepage](/). So far the videos have over 2,300 views.

Recent and upcoming speakers come from NEAR, Pramaana Labs, HigherOrderCO, o1Labs, and academia. See [past talks](/#past-session-conversation-topics) for the full list.

## What sponsorship covers

| Package | What it pays for | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **Food** | Pizza and drinks every week for a quarter (about 13 meetups) | $3,000 / quarter |
| **Video** | Professional editing for every talk video in a quarter | $2,500 / quarter |
| **Full** | Both of the above | $5,500 / quarter |
| **Single night** | Food for one meetup | $300 |

These amounts are what the meetup actually costs to run. If it's easier, you can pay for food directly instead of sending money: for example, a weekly order on your company's DoorDash, ezCater, or Uber Eats account, delivered to Mox.

## What sponsors get

- **Your logo on every talk video** posted during the sponsored period.
- **A mention on the Luma event page** and from the front of the room each week ("Food tonight is courtesy of …").
- **A few minutes at the start of the night**, if you'd like, to talk about what your team is working on or that you're hiring.
- **A listing on this website** for the sponsored period.

## What stays the same

- **Talks are chosen by the organizers.** Sponsors don't pick or approve speakers or topics.
- **Sponsorship isn't exclusive.** We may have more than one sponsor at a time, and companies working in the same area are welcome. Everyone is credited equally.
- **We don't share attendee contact details.**
